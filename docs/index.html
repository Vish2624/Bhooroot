How It Started

From a Farm in Dindigul
to All of India

Uhazvumart was born out of frustration. Our founders watched small-scale farmers pay 30–40% more than MRP for essential agro inputs because local dealers controlled supply and pricing.

In 2020, we launched a simple idea: connect farmers directly with CIB&RC-licensed manufacturers and cut out every middleman. No markups, no fake products, no compromise.

Today we serve over 12,000 farmers across 28 states — from paddy fields in Tamil Nadu to wheat farms in Punjab — and we're just getting started.
