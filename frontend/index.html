How It Started

From a Farm in Dindigul
to All of India

Uhazvumart was born out of frustration. Our founders watched small-scale farmers pay 30–40% more than MRP for essential agro inputs because local dealers controlled supply and pricing.

In 2020, we launched a simple idea: connect farmers directly with CIB&RC-licensed manufacturers and cut out every middleman. No markups, no fake products, no compromise.

Today we serve over 12,000 farmers across 28 states — from paddy fields in Tamil Nadu to wheat farms in Punjab — and we're just getting started.

Hassle-Free Returns

Return Policy

We stand behind every product we sell. If something isn't right, we'll make it right.

7-Day Return Window

You may return eligible items within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unused, in original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase.

Eligible Items

  • Defective or damaged products on arrival
  • Wrong item delivered
  • Sealed products that are unopened
  • Farm machinery with manufacturing defects

Non-Returnable Items

  • Opened seed packets or fertiliser bags
  • Pesticides and chemicals once opened
  • Perishable or temperature-sensitive items
  • Items without original packaging

Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, your refund will be processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method. UPI refunds are typically faster.

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact us at sales@uhazvumart.in or call +91 98765 43210
  2. Share your Order ID and reason for return
  3. Our team will arrange pickup within 48 hours
  4. Refund processed after quality check
